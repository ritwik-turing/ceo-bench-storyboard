Brand and production notes

This is an internal planning storyboard for the 5-act demo. Its visual treatment uses Turing’s primary font and approved logo. The frames describe the intended production experience; they are not screenshots of completed features.

Applied brand rules

  • Full, unmodified Turing logo from the linked official asset folder, shown at 120px minimum with clear surrounding space.
  • Poppins regular and semibold throughout this internal artifact. No serif display font or APK Galeria/Poppins mixture.
  • Black, white, and gray foundations; selective blue accents. Dark blue #003F9B for readable small text and actions; blue #317EFF for non-text emphasis; light blue #EEFBFF for supporting panels.
  • Sentence-case headings, labels, and actions. Numerals by default, Oxford commas, expanded technical terms, and no em dashes.
  • Direct, evidence-led narration, with sources and dates adjacent to benchmark claims. No unsupported promises about model failure, training gains, or contamination.

Typography scope

The July documents restrict APK Galeria to external marketing headings and also prohibit mixing it with Poppins, without specifying an external body font. This storyboard uses the unambiguous internal Poppins system. An external advertising treatment still needs that font decision resolved. The linked external-design file was not accessible through the connected Drive account.

Source hierarchy

The content playbook is dated July 30, 2026; the logo guide is updated July 2026. The broad “2026” guide has a 2025 cover. Where writing rules conflict, this storyboard follows the later playbook. This is a documented editorial choice.

Keep these distinctions visible

  • Published results: the 20.3% all-core figure is a dated snapshot, distinct from weighted reward. Refresh before the event.
  • Recorded model attempt: requires an actual run artifact with model, harness, task version, and run date. Newly written criteria receive manual evidence review.
  • Prototype rehearsal: illustrative packets, authored attempts, and annotated outcomes stay labeled. No model is called.
  • Single-laptop operation: parallel paper work and sequential capture. Separate laptops require a shared session service.
  • Reference answers and rubrics: show on screen only under the supplied event rules. Hand out only prompts, public-source files, and blank worksheets.

Production gaps

Approved source packets, file/photo capture, a genuine recorded attempt, and a “needs clarification” grading state are required for the full storyboard. The current prototype supports typed memos, same-browser submissions, manual pass/fail, fixture annotations, and a synchronized audience view. It has not been restyled by this storyboard deliverable.
